WhyIQ Plugin for AI Coding Assistants (v1.1.1)
================================================

What WhyIQ Is
-------------
WhyIQ (https://www.whyiq.ai) is a conversion rate optimization (CRO) and AI
visibility platform built around two separate engines:

1. WhyIQ Page Scanner
   Simulates 50 realistic buyer personas reviewing a live landing page to
   predict Clarity, CTA Pull (scored as an integer from 0 to 100), and
   heuristic on-page AI recommendation readiness, then ranks exact fixes.

2. WhyIQ AI Radar
   Measures weekly AI visibility (the share of AI answers that name you as
   Mentioned or link to you as Cited) across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI
   Overviews, Google AI Mode, Gemini, and Claude.


What This GitHub Repository Is
------------------------------
This repository (https://github.com/block9team/whyiq-plugin) is the official,
MIT-licensed public plugin that connects AI coding assistants (including
Antigravity, Cursor, Claude Code, and Gemini CLI) to your WhyIQ account via
a remote Model Context Protocol (MCP) server at https://www.whyiq.ai/api/mcp.

It contains only public plugin manifests, agent skills, visual card
templates, safety rules, and MCP configuration files. All proprietary
simulation engines, crawler pipelines, and customer account data remain on
WhyIQ's servers and are accessed only through your OAuth 2.1 session.


Why This Plugin Exists
----------------------
Finding conversion blockers or missed AI answers in a web dashboard still
leaves developers copying notes back into a code editor by hand. That manual
handoff wastes time, introduces guesswork, and risks rewriting sections that
AI engines already cite.

The WhyIQ plugin brings live scan findings, ranked code fixes, and citation
preservation rules directly into the editor where you work, while linking back
to https://www.whyiq.ai whenever you need to launch a new scan, review
off-site drafts, or manage your workspace.


What the Plugin Does
--------------------
- Pulls a unified overview of your verified domain, recent Page Scanner scores,
  and weekly AI Radar visibility into your coding assistant.
- Explains specific visitor objections from 50-persona runs alongside the CTA
  Pull and Clarity drivers behind them.
- Surfaces weekly AI visibility gaps from AI Radar so you can see which buyer
  questions competitors win.
- Stages or applies guarded code fixes according to your Switchboard mode
  and records before/after snapshots for 1-click rollback.
- Keeps off-site community and review drafts (such as Reddit or review sites)
  held for human review so nothing is posted automatically.


How Safety and Privacy Work
---------------------------
1. OAuth 2.1 + PKCE Sign-In
   When an MCP client connects to https://www.whyiq.ai/api/mcp without a token,
   WhyIQ responds with HTTP 401 Unauthorized and standard OAuth discovery
   headers. You authenticate in your browser on whyiq.ai, select the domain
   and Switchboard preset your assistant may use, and grant scoped access.

2. Honesty Gate ([fill in: ...] Placeholders)
   The plugin forbids guessing customer counts, case study results, pricing,
   SLAs, security certifications, or testimonials. When real business facts
   are missing, your assistant pauses and asks you to supply them first.

3. 4-Mode Switchboard
   Every fix category follows your chosen mode:
   - off: Do not surface or touch items in this category.
   - flag: Report findings as read-only intelligence.
   - stage: Prepare a diff or preview for explicit approval first.
   - auto: Apply directly once a ledger snapshot is captured and zero
     unresolved [fill in: ...] placeholders remain.

4. Ledger Snapshots and Preservation
   Before editing files, the assistant calls whyiq_prepare_fix_bundle so WhyIQ
   captures a before snapshot, SHA-256 hash, and keepAnswers list (passages
   already cited in AI Radar). After edits finish, it records an after snapshot
   via whyiq_verify_and_record_fix so 1-click rollback stays intact.

5. Measurement vs. Prediction Separation
   Weekly AI Radar visibility (Mentioned or Cited share across real AI engines)
   is never conflated with heuristic Page Scanner predictions or 50-persona
   CTA Pull scores.
